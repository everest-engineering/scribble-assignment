# Quickstart: Result, Restart & Final Validation

## Validation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Reveal Word on End
- Game transitions to `results` status.
- Verify that both drawer and guessers see the correct word and final scores on screen.

### Scenario 2: Host Restart
- Host clicks "Restart Game".
- Verify that room transitions back to `lobby` state.
- Verify player scores are reset to 0, and all participant list details (like usernames) remain unchanged.
